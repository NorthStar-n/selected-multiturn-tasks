# July copper qualification runbook

Scope is every July 2026 copper conductor material identity or catalog comparator referenced by supplier intake, lab reports, catalog snapshots, or the literature pull. Archive lots remain traceability context and do not enter the release count.

For lot release, use the current effective control-plan revision. Apply a later change notice only to the named clause and stack family. Apply the strictest applicable impurity limit across the three published standards and any customer addendum. Documented waivers must be identified by approval author, date, and waiver identifier.

For material properties, use measured values where available; flag literature-only values as unverified. Test-condition mismatches must stay in the evidence register but must not replace a condition-matched measurement.

The source register is the first workpaper. The model, readout, and review memo should reuse its material IDs and evidence anchors rather than creating new labels.
